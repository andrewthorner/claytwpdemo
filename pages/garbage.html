Trash & Recycling Services

Clayton Township provides weekly curbside trash collection for township residents. Review pickup schedules, accepted item guidelines, holiday adjustments, and local landfill contacts below.

Curbside Collection Schedule & Rules

Weekly Pickup Schedule

Collection Day: Every Thursday morning

Set-Out Time: Please have your trash containers placed at the roadside by 6:00 AM on Thursday or the evening before.

* Collection times may vary slightly during extreme weather or seasonal road conditions.

Container & Bag Rules

Bagging Requirement: All domestic household waste must be bagged and placed inside approved, covered trash receptacles to prevent wind scattering and wildlife attraction.

Weight Limit: Individual containers or bags must not exceed 50 lbs.

Holiday Collection Delays

When an official holiday falls on or before Thursday during a calendar week, collection services will be delayed by one day (moving pickup to Friday).

Observed Holidays: New Year's Day, Memorial Day, 4th of July, Labor Day, Thanksgiving Day, and Christmas Day.

Acceptable & Prohibited Materials

Prohibited Waste Items

The following items are NOT permitted in weekly curbside household trash:

  • Hazardous chemicals, liquid paint, & oils
  • Car batteries & lithium-ion electronics
  • Yards waste, brush, compost, & tree stumps
  • Construction debris, concrete, & roofing shingles
  • Tires & heavy vehicle scrap parts

Bulk Items & Appliance Disposal

Large household items (such as furniture, mattresses, or appliances) require special handling or direct disposal at local transfer stations.

For questions regarding bulk item stickers or special pickup arrangements, please contact the Township Clerk or Waste Management directly.
