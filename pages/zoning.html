Digital Accessibility & Document Assistance

Clayton Township is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for all residents. Due to the complex visual nature of zoning maps, parcel diagrams, and technical planning charts within these documents, some content may present barriers for screen reader software.

If you require assistance accessing, reading, or interpreting any portion of these documents, please contact the Township Clerk at (989) 274-8770 or by email at claytontwpclerk@gmail.com to request an accessible alternative or verbal summary.
