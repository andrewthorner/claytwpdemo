Digital Accessibility & Financial Record Assistance

Clayton Township strives to ensure digital accessibility for all community members. Because municipal budget worksheets contain extensive multi-column financial tables and account ledger codes[cite: 3], these PDF documents may present navigation challenges for screen reader software.

If you require assistance reading, interpreting, or requesting an accessible alternative format of township financial records, please contact the Township Clerk at (989) 274-8770 or by email at claytontwpclerk@gmail.com.
