Property Tax Information

The Township Treasurer collects property taxes for local operations, schools, and Arenac County services. Review payment deadlines, drop box locations, and essential State of Michigan tax exemption forms below.

Payment Deadlines & Instructions

Important Due Dates

Summer Taxes: Due September 14th

Winter Taxes: Due February 14th

* Taxes are billed for the current fiscal year. Delinquent tax payments from prior years must be paid directly through the Arenac County Treasurer.

Payment Methods & Drop Box

Accepted Forms: Check or Money Order (exact amounts required; cash is not accepted by mail).

Mailing & Drop Box Address:
3960 Main Street Road, Sterling, MI 48659

Important Drop Box Location Note: Please use the drop box at 3960 Main Street Road. Do not drop tax payments off at the Township Hall drop box.

Tax Forms & Exemption Applications

Download official State Tax Commission and Michigan Department of Treasury forms required for property transfers, exemptions, and appeals.

  • Principal Residence Exemption (PRE) Affidavit (Form 2368) – Claim exemption on your primary residence to reduce local school operating taxes.
  • Request to Rescind Principal Residence Exemption (Form 2602) – File when selling or converting your primary residence.
  • Property Transfer Affidavit (Form 2766 / L-4260) – Required to be filed within 45 days of any property purchase or title transfer.
  • Disabled Veterans Exemption Affidavit (Form 5107) – Application for complete property tax exemption for eligible disabled veterans and unremarried surviving spouses.
  • Application for Deferment of Summer Property Taxes – Defer summer property taxes without penalty for eligible senior citizens, disabled individuals, or agricultural property owners.
  • Farmland Exemption Application (Form 3676) – Exemption claims for qualified agricultural properties.
  • Petition to Board of Review (Form L-4035) – Submit an official protest to the March Board of Review regarding property assessments.
  • Michigan Tax Tribunal Appeals – Information and petition forms for appealing Board of Review decisions.
