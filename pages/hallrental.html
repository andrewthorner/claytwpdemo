Facility Overview & Rental Information

Host your next occasion at the Clayton Township Hall! Offering over 1,000 square feet of versatile space, our facility provides an ideal setting for everything from family birthday celebrations and graduation parties to formal wedding receptions and community meetings.

Residency Notice: Clayton Township Hall rentals are available exclusively to current Clayton Township residents.

Included Hall Amenities

Spacious Event Floor

Over 1,000 square feet of open room capacity. Banquet tables and standard seating chairs are provided on-site for your event arrangement.

Full Kitchen Facility

Equipped with two full cooking ranges, a large refrigerator for food storage, and a deep sink setup for easy meal preparation and cleanup.

Guest Accommodations

Features a dedicated coatroom for guest attire alongside clean, fully accessible gender-separated restroom facilities.
